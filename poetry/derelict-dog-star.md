**derelict dog star**

::: {.poetry}
just beyond <br>
the stratosphere<br>
&emsp;lies <br>

a memory<br>
&emsp;of a stray <br>
sacrificed in pursuit <br>
&emsp;&emsp;of Science<br>

the last vestiges<br>
&emsp;of little Laika<br>
howling <br>
&emsp;for <br>

&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Home. <br><br><br>

:::
