**future**

::: {.poetry}
How cruel it is<br>
that tomorrow always comes<br><br>

that no matter how we love&mdash;<br>
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;carelessly, <br><br>

&emsp;&emsp;or <br>
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;with all our heart&mdash;<br><br>

all <br>
&emsp;that <br>
&emsp;&emsp;begins <br>
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;will someday <br>

END<br><br><br>

:::
